Why We Started

The Case for Boutique

TradeTech Search Group was founded by two recruiting professionals with 12+ years of combined experience placing engineering talent at one of the largest staffing firms in the country. We saw firsthand what happens when recruiting becomes a volume game — candidates treated as transactions, clients handed off between reps, and niche technical roles filled by generalists who don't understand the work.

We built TradeTech to do it differently. As a boutique firm focused exclusively on Automation, AI, Robotics, and Engineering talent, every search gets our full attention. We don't have competing priorities. We don't have junior reps learning on your requisition. We know this niche because we've spent years in it.

Our model is direct placement — contingency and retained search — for companies that need the right hire, not just a fast one. We operate across the Northeast with deep networks in Greater Boston, Connecticut, Providence, and NYC Metro.

How We Operate

Our Principles

01
Niche Over Volume

We only work Automation, AI, Robotics, and Engineering searches. That focus means every candidate we know and every client relationship we build is relevant to what you need.

02
Founders on Every Search

You work directly with the people who built the firm. No handoffs to junior recruiters, no account management layers. The person you talk to is the person doing the work.

03
Candidates Are Clients

We treat every engineer we work with as a long-term relationship, not a transaction. That's how we build the kind of talent network that produces results other firms can't replicate.

04
Speed Without Compromise

Boutique doesn't mean slow. Our focused network means we can move faster than larger firms with broader but shallower coverage.

05
Market Intelligence

We share what we're seeing — salary data, candidate availability, competitor hiring signals — because informed clients make better hiring decisions and better partners.

06
Honest Over Comfortable

If a search isn't working, we say so. If a candidate isn't the right fit, we tell you. Honest conversations early save everyone time and money.
